Privacy policy

This policy covers the Android apps Ketabkhan (package com.example.ketabkhan) and SubtitleReader (package com.example.subtitlereader). Back to the home page.

What stays on your phone

By default everything happens on your phone: the files, photos and videos you open, the text found in them, the audiobooks and subtitle files the apps create, notes, bookmarks and settings. The apps have no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers of their own. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.

Optional: Google Drive text recognition

In the settings you can choose to let Google Drive recognise text. This is off by default. If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account and allow one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With this permission the app can only see files that the app itself creates in your own Google Drive.

When this option is on, the app sends a picture (a page photo, a PDF page, or a picture of a subtitle line) to your own Google Drive, where Google converts it to a Google Doc. This is how Google reads the text. The app then reads the text of that document and immediately deletes the temporary file. The text is kept only on your phone. The pictures and text are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Google's handling of the pictures while they are in your Drive is covered by Google's own privacy policy. You can turn the option off at any time in the settings, and you can remove the app's access in your Google Account settings under security and third-party access.

Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use the Drive permission to provide the text recognition feature described above. We do not use it for advertising, we do not transfer it to others, and no person reads it.

Other permissions

Microphone: only to record voice notes you create in Ketabkhan; the recordings stay on your phone. Internet: only for the optional Google Drive feature. Notifications: to keep the app running while a book or subtitle is being prepared.

Children

The apps do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. For questions, contact the owner of the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
